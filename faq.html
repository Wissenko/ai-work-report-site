FAQ

Questions before you get your AI Work Plan.

The report is designed for regular office workers who want to know where AI fits their actual job — not a technical training program, generic prompt pack, or tool directory.

What happens after I click “Get my AI Work Plan”?

You start with a short voice interview about your real work. After the interview, you can pay $9.99 to receive your personal AI Work Plan by email.

When do I pay?

You pay after the interview, before the personal report is delivered. Your AI Work Plan is a one-time $9.99 purchase, not a subscription.

What information should I avoid sharing?

Avoid names, client details, private employee information, confidential numbers, passwords, legal matters, medical information, regulated data, and anything your company policy does not allow.

Can I use fake names or general examples?

Yes. You can say “a coworker,” “a vendor,” “a customer,” or “an internal project.” The report can still work without sensitive details.

Who can see my report?

Your report is delivered by email as a link. Treat that link as private if your interview includes work context you would not want shared.

Will this tell me my job is being replaced?

No. The report is not a prediction about whether your job will disappear. It is a practical plan for where AI may help with parts of your work today, and where your judgment, review, and decisions still matter.

What if I am not very technical?

That is fine. The interview is designed for regular office workers. You do not need to know AI terms. You just talk about your role, your tasks, your tools, and the work that takes time, repeats often, or feels risky to get wrong.

Is this an AI course?

No. Your AI Work Plan is a personal report. You answer questions about your work, and the report shows where AI may fit your actual job. It is not a class, certification, or long training program.

How long is my audio or transcript kept?

Audio and transcript data are used to generate your report and support the service. You can request deletion of your interview data and report using the contact email provided at payment or report delivery.

Can I delete my interview and report?

You can request deletion of your interview and report data. Use the contact email provided at payment or report delivery.

What if my company does not allow AI tools?

Follow your company policy. Your report can suggest safer starting points and non-sensitive examples, but it does not override workplace rules, manager guidance, privacy rules, or approved-tool requirements.

Will the report recommend tools I have to buy?

Not necessarily. The report starts with tool categories and tools you may already have access to, such as a general AI assistant, spreadsheet assistant, task system, workspace assistant, or company-approved AI tool.

What if I do not like the report?

The report is generated from your interview, so it may not match every preference. Refunds are for technical issues that prevent report generation or delivery.
