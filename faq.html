FAQ

Questions before you start.

The report is designed for regular office workers who want a practical AI starting point, not a technical training program.

Do I need to know anything about AI first?

No. The interview is designed for people who do not want jargon. You just talk about your job, your repeated tasks, the tools you use, and where you want help.

Is this an AI course?

No. Your AI Work Plan is a personal report. It is not a course, certification, prompt library, or long training program.

Who is this for?

It is for regular office workers who are starting to feel that AI is becoming a workplace skill.

What do I get?

You get a personal AI Work Plan showing where AI fits your work, what to try first, which tools or tool categories to use, what to put into them, what prompts to use, and what you should still check or decide yourself.

What is the difference between free role guides, sample reports, and the paid report?

Role guides are free general advice for common office jobs. Sample reports show example artifacts for specific roles. The paid report is based on your own interview, tasks, tools, examples, and risk points.

How long does it take?

The voice interview takes a few minutes. Your report link is sent by email after the interview.

How do I pay?

The report is a one-time $9.99 purchase. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by this site.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is a one-time payment for one personal AI Work Plan.

What happens to my audio?

Your interview audio may be collected so the report can be generated. Avoid sharing highly sensitive information. The report can still be useful if you use anonymized examples like “a client,” “a teammate,” or “an internal project.”

Do you keep a transcript?

A transcript may be created from your interview and used to generate your report. If generation uses third-party AI systems, your transcript may be processed to create the report.

Can I request deletion?

Yes. You can request deletion of your interview and report data using the contact email provided on checkout or report delivery pages. The current privacy contact is privacy@heylemma.com.

Will my answers be used as anonymized examples?

The report can work with anonymized examples. Do not share names, private customer data, confidential financial details, or other sensitive information unless your organization allows it.

What if my company does not allow AI tools?

Follow your company policy. If you are not allowed to use AI tools for certain work, do not use them for that work.

Should I follow my company policy?

Yes. Your AI Work Plan can suggest safer starting points, but it does not override workplace rules, privacy requirements, or manager guidance.

What tools might you recommend?

The report may recommend common AI tools or tool types that fit your work, such as writing assistants, spreadsheet help, meeting note tools, summarizers, or tools already available in your workplace.

Will this tell me my job is being replaced?

No. The report is not a prediction about whether your job will disappear. It is a practical plan for where AI can help with parts of your work today and where your judgment still matters.

Is it safe to use AI for work?

It depends on the task, the tool, and the information involved. That is why the report includes privacy, quality, and review cautions.

What if my work is sensitive?

The report will help flag sensitive areas where you should be careful. You should also follow your company policies.

What if I already use ChatGPT or Copilot?

This can still help. The report helps turn scattered use into a clearer plan: what to use AI for, what prompts to use, what to check, and what to try for one week.

What if the report is not useful?

If we cannot generate a useful report from your interview, you can request a refund.
