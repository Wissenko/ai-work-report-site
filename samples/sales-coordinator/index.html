YOUR AI WORK PLAN

Role: Sales Coordinator / Account Coordinator

Your best first AI test

Turn call notes and email threads into a CRM update and follow-up checklist.

This is a strong first test because sales coordination depends on clean handoffs, accurate next steps, and careful customer-facing language.

01 · READ THIS FIRST

Your best first AI test is turning sales notes into a CRM update and follow-up checklist.

Based on this sample interview, your best first AI test is turning call notes and email threads into a CRM update and follow-up checklist.

This is the right place to start because:

  • Sales follow-ups are frequent.
  • Details get scattered between calls, email, and CRM.
  • The output is easy to review.
  • AI can draft and organize without deciding pricing or strategy.
  • Missed follow-ups are visible.
Use AI for

Summarizing customer requests, drafting follow-up notes, preparing CRM updates, and marking missing information.

Keep yourself

Pricing, promises, account strategy, contract details, customer-sensitive information, and final wording.

This week, your goal is

Use AI on one safe account follow-up and compare the output with your normal CRM update.

05 · WHAT NOT TO HAND OVER

Before using AI, keep these boundaries clear.

Boundary

Pricing

AI can organize notes. Do not let it decide or imply pricing.

Why it matters here: Pricing changes customer expectations.

Boundary

Customer promises

AI can draft follow-ups. Do not let it promise timelines, deliverables, or contract terms.

Why it matters here: Written language can become a commitment.

Boundary

Account strategy

AI can summarize context. Do not let it decide the strategy or next move.

Why it matters here: Sales context depends on human judgment.

Boundary

Sensitive customer details

AI can work from safe summaries. Do not paste sensitive account details unless approved.

Why it matters here: Customer data can be sensitive.

06 · YOUR FIRST TEST

Turn call notes and email threads into a CRM update and follow-up checklist.

The goal

Make account follow-ups clearer without relying on memory or scattered notes.

Tools

Use a writing assistant to organize notes. Use your CRM or normal task system for the reviewed update.

If your company already provides an approved AI tool, start there. If not, use only safe, non-sensitive examples until you know the policy.

Steps

  1. Choose one safe account or opportunity.
  2. Collect call notes and relevant email notes.
  3. Remove sensitive pricing, contract, or customer details if needed.
  4. Ask AI for a CRM-ready summary and follow-up checklist.
  5. Review every commitment before updating the CRM.
Success looks like
  • CRM notes are easier to write.
  • Open questions are visible.
  • Follow-ups have owners and dates.
  • No pricing or customer promise is invented.
First-test rule

Keep the first test narrow, safe, and easy to review before using it on higher-risk work.

07 · WHAT THE TOOL NEEDS FROM YOU

AI will only be useful here if you give it the right raw material.

For the writing assistant, prepare
  • Safe call notes
  • Customer request summary
  • Known next steps
  • Internal owners
  • Deal stage if already confirmed
  • What not to promise
For the CRM or task system, prepare
  • Final account summary
  • Next step
  • Owner
  • Due date
  • Open question
  • Follow-up note
Weak input

“Summarize this sales call.”

Better input

Turn these safe sales notes into a CRM update and follow-up checklist. Do not invent pricing, deal stage, timelines, or customer commitments.

Notes:
- Customer asked about [request]
- Current confirmed stage: [stage]
- Follow-up needed: [follow-up]
- Internal owner: [role]
- Open question: [question]

Leave out

  • Contract terms
  • Sensitive pricing
  • Confidential customer details
  • Internal strategy not approved for AI
  • Personal contact data not needed for the task

08 · WHAT YOU STILL OWN

The goal is not to hand your work to AI.

The goal is to stop doing the parts AI can prepare for you.

Responsibility

Pricing

AI can organize notes. You still own anything related to price or discount.

Responsibility

Promises

AI can draft follow-ups. You still own timelines, deliverables, and commitments.

Responsibility

CRM accuracy

AI can suggest updates. You still own whether CRM fields are correct.

Responsibility

Account context

AI can summarize facts. You still own the meaning of the account context.

Responsibility

Final wording

AI can draft. You still own customer-facing language.

09 · YOUR PROMPTS AND TEMPLATES

Copy the prompt that matches the task you are starting with.

Replace anything in square brackets before you send.

Prompt 1 — Sales notes to CRM update

Role/context: I support sales coordination and need to turn call notes and email threads into clean CRM updates. Task: Create a CRM-ready summary and follow-up checklist. Input: Account or opportunity: [name or placeholder] Confirmed stage: [stage] Safe notes: [notes] Known next steps: [steps] Open questions: [questions] Constraints: - Do not invent pricing, deal stage, timelines, or commitments. - Separate confirmed facts from questions. - Keep the wording clear and concise. - Do not include sensitive customer details. Output format: Create sections for CRM summary, next steps, owners, due dates, open questions, and what I should verify. Before you finish: List anything that could create a customer promise.

Prompt 2 — Customer follow-up draft

Role/context: I help prepare sales follow-up messages. Task: Draft a customer follow-up email using only approved facts. Input: Customer context: [safe summary] Approved facts: [facts] Next step: [step] Tone: [professional, helpful, concise] Constraints: - Do not mention pricing unless provided. - Do not promise timelines or deliverables. - Do not add details not in the input. - Keep it concise. Output format: Give me one draft email and a check-before-sending list. Before you finish: Tell me what I should confirm with the salesperson before sending.

10 · YOUR 7-DAY PLAN

Use one safe workflow for one week.

Day 1

Pick one safe account follow-up.

Day 2

Run the CRM update prompt.

Day 3

Review commitments, stage, owners, and dates.

Day 4

Move reviewed notes into the CRM.

Day 5

Try one customer follow-up draft with approved facts.

Day 6

Compare against your normal CRM notes.

Day 7

Decide whether to use this for weekly follow-up cleanup.

If it works

Next, test proposal checklists and internal handoff summaries.

If it does not work

The likely issue is missing context. Add confirmed stage, owner, and next-step fields.

Optional manager-safe explanation

“I’m testing AI to organize safe sales notes into CRM updates and follow-up checklists. I’m not using it for pricing, account strategy, customer promises, or contract terms.”
