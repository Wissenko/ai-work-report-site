01 · READ THIS FIRST

Your best first AI test is turning spreadsheet notes into a report outline and review checklist.

Based on this sample interview, your best first AI test is turning spreadsheet notes into a report outline and review checklist.

This is the right place to start because:

  • Reporting work repeats.
  • The hardest part is often organizing what the numbers might mean.
  • The output is reviewable.
  • AI can help find questions, not make final conclusions.
  • You can use placeholders or safe data summaries.
Use AI for

Keep yourself

Source checking, numbers, assumptions, analysis, recommendations, and final conclusions.

Use AI on one safe report draft and check whether it helps you ask better questions before finalizing.

05 · WHAT NOT TO HAND OVER

Before using AI, keep these boundaries clear.

Boundary

Numbers

AI can organize notes. Do not let it decide whether a number is correct.

Why it matters here: Reports depend on source accuracy.

Boundary

Sources

AI can list sources to check. Do not use it as the source of truth.

Why it matters here: Your report must trace back to real data.

Boundary

Recommendations

AI can draft options. Do not let it make the final recommendation.

Why it matters here: Recommendations depend on context and accountability.

Boundary

Confidential data

AI can work from summaries or placeholders. Do not paste sensitive data unless approved.

Why it matters here: Reporting may include sensitive business data.

06 · YOUR FIRST TEST

Turn spreadsheet notes into a report outline, variance questions, and review checklist.

The goal

Prepare a better report draft without letting AI decide what the numbers mean.

Tools

Use a writing assistant for structure, questions, and draft language. Use your spreadsheet tool for verified data and formulas.

If your company already provides an approved AI tool, start there. If not, use only safe, non-sensitive examples until you know the policy.

Steps

  1. Choose one safe report or spreadsheet summary.
  2. Write short notes about what changed.
  3. Ask AI for an outline, variance questions, and review checklist.
  4. Check every number and source yourself.
  5. Use only the parts that help your final report.
Success looks like
  • Your report outline is clearer.
  • You have better questions to investigate.
  • The summary draft is easier to edit.
  • No claim is used without checking the data.
First-test rule

Keep the first test narrow, safe, and easy to review before using it on higher-risk work.

07 · WHAT THE TOOL NEEDS FROM YOU

AI will only be useful here if you give it the right raw material.

For the writing assistant, prepare
  • Report purpose
  • Audience
  • Safe notes about changes
  • Verified numbers or placeholders
  • Known assumptions
  • Questions you already have
For the spreadsheet, prepare
  • Source tabs
  • Metric definitions
  • Date ranges
  • Formulas to verify
  • Known data quality issues
Weak input

“Explain this spreadsheet.”

Better input

Help me turn these safe spreadsheet notes into a report outline and review checklist. Do not invent causes or conclusions. Mark anything I need to verify.

Report purpose: [purpose]
Audience: [audience]
Metrics: [metrics]
Observed changes: [changes]
Known assumptions: [assumptions]

Leave out

  • Confidential financial data
  • Customer-identifying data
  • Employee data
  • Unapproved source files
  • Sensitive business plans

08 · WHAT YOU STILL OWN

The goal is not to hand your work to AI.

The goal is to stop doing the parts AI can prepare for you.

Responsibility

Source checking

AI can organize questions. You still own whether the source is correct.

Responsibility

Numbers

AI can summarize notes. You still own every number in the report.

Responsibility

Assumptions

AI can list possible assumptions. You still own which assumptions are valid.

Responsibility

Recommendation

AI can draft language. You still own the final recommendation.

Responsibility

Final explanation

AI can prepare a first draft. You still own what the report actually says.

09 · YOUR PROMPTS AND TEMPLATES

Copy the prompt that matches the task you are starting with.

Replace anything in square brackets before you send.

Prompt 1 — Spreadsheet notes to report outline

Role/context: I prepare reports and analysis using spreadsheets, source data, and manager requests. Task: Turn my safe spreadsheet notes into a report outline, variance questions, and review checklist. Input: Report purpose: [purpose] Audience: [audience] Metrics: [metrics] Observed changes: [changes] Known assumptions: [assumptions] Questions I already have: [questions] Constraints: - Do not invent causes or conclusions. - Separate confirmed facts from questions. - Mark anything I need to verify. - Do not use sensitive data. - Do not make the final recommendation. Output format: Create sections for report outline, variance questions, possible checks, draft summary, and what I should verify. Before you finish: List every number, source, assumption, and conclusion I should check.

Prompt 2 — Formula explanation

Role/context: I work with spreadsheets and need to understand formula logic. Task: Explain this formula and suggest what I should test. Input: Formula: [formula] What it should calculate: [purpose] Sample safe values: [values] Constraints: - Explain in plain English. - Do not assume the formula is correct. - List possible failure cases. - Do not use confidential data. Output format: Give me a plain-English explanation, possible issues, and tests to run. Before you finish: Tell me what I should verify in the spreadsheet.

10 · YOUR 7-DAY PLAN

Test one useful AI workflow for one week.

Day 1

Choose one safe report or spreadsheet summary.

Day 2

Run the report outline prompt.

Day 3

Check every number, source, and assumption.

Day 4

Use AI to draft a short summary from verified notes.

Day 5

Ask AI for questions to investigate, not conclusions.

Day 6

Compare the AI-assisted outline with your normal process.

Day 7

Decide whether to use this before recurring reports.

If it works

Next, test variance explanation drafts and data cleanup checklists.

If it does not work

The likely issue is that the input was too broad. Give AI the report purpose, audience, metrics, and known changes.

Optional manager-safe explanation

“I’m testing AI to structure report notes and create review questions. I’m not using it as the source of truth or for final recommendations.”
