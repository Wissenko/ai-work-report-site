Privacy

Privacy for your AI Work Plan.

This page explains what information is used to generate your report, what not to share, and how to request deletion.

What we collect

To create your report, the service may collect your interview audio, transcript, email address, generated report, and basic payment confirmation.

Payment card details are processed by our payment provider. We do not store your full card number.

How the information is used

  • To run the voice interview.
  • To generate your personal report.
  • To email you the report link.
  • To handle refunds or support requests.
  • To improve the report format and interview questions.

What not to share

Do not share highly sensitive information in the interview unless you are allowed to do so. This includes client secrets, private employee information, passwords, confidential financial details, legal matters, medical information, or regulated data.

The report can still work with anonymized examples. You can say "a client," "a teammate," or "an internal project" instead of naming people or companies.

Use of external tools

The report may reference public AI tools and publicly available product pages. If generation uses third-party AI systems, your audio transcript may be processed to create the report.

You should not paste sensitive company or client data into external tools unless your organization allows it.

Report link

Your report is delivered by email as a link. Treat the link as private if the interview contains work context you would not want shared publicly.

Deletion requests

You can request deletion of your interview audio, transcript, generated report, and email address where required. Use the contact email provided on the checkout or report delivery page.

No selling personal data

Your AI Work Plan should not sell your personal data. The purpose of this paid test is to generate your report and improve the report experience.

Refunds

If a technical issue prevents report generation or delivery, the paid report is refunded.
