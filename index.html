Questions

Questions before you start

The report is designed for regular office workers who want a practical AI starting point, not a technical training program.

Do I need to know anything about AI first?

No. The interview is designed for people who do not want jargon. You just talk about your job, your repeated tasks, the tools you use, and where you want help.

Is this an AI course?

No. Your AI Work Plan is a personal report. It is not a course, certification, prompt library, or long training program.

Who is this for?

It is for regular office workers who are starting to feel that AI is becoming a workplace skill. That includes administrative assistants, office managers, HR coordinators, finance and accounting associates, customer support reps, operations coordinators, sales coordinators, junior analysts, and similar roles.

What do I get?

You get a personal AI Work Plan showing where AI fits your work, what to try first, which tools or tool categories to use, what to put into them, what prompts to use, and what you should still check or decide yourself.

How long does it take?

The voice interview takes a few minutes. Your report link is sent by email within a few minutes after the interview.

What tools might you recommend?

The report may recommend common AI tools or tool types that fit your work, such as writing assistants, spreadsheet help, meeting note tools, summarizers, or tools already available in your workplace. The goal is not to give you a giant tool list. The goal is to show what makes sense for your job.

Why is the report based on a voice interview?

Voice makes it easier to explain the messy details of your work. The interview can ask follow-up questions and use those details to build a more specific report.

Will this tell me my job is being replaced?

No. The report is not a prediction about whether your job will disappear. It is a practical plan for where AI can help with parts of your work today and where your judgment still matters.

Is it safe to use AI for work?

It depends on the task, the tool, and the information involved. That is why the report includes privacy, quality, and review cautions. It helps you think about what not to paste into tools, what needs checking, and what should stay your decision.

What if my work is sensitive?

The report will help flag sensitive areas where you should be careful. You should also follow your company’s policies. If you are not allowed to use AI tools for certain work, do not use them for that work.

Do I need an account?

You do not need a long setup process to start the interview. You will need to provide an email address so your report link can be sent to you.

What if I already use ChatGPT or Copilot?

This can still help. Many people have tried AI but only use it randomly. The report helps turn scattered use into a clearer plan: what to use AI for, what prompts to use, what to check, and what to try for one week.

What if the report is not useful?

If we cannot generate a useful report from your interview, you can request a refund.
